FAQ

Pricing Questions

Can I switch plans at any time?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and we'll prorate the difference.

Is there a free trial for paid plans?

Yes, all paid plans come with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required. You get full access to all features during the trial.

Do you offer annual billing?

Yes, annual billing is available at a 20% discount on all plans. Contact our sales team for details.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, ACH transfers, and can issue invoices for annual Enterprise contracts.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits?

Yes. Qualified nonprofits receive 30% off any plan. Contact us with your organization details to apply.

Need a Custom Quote?

For organizations with unique requirements, volume licensing, or multi-product bundles, our team can create a custom proposal.
